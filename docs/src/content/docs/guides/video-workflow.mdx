This guide covers the complete workflow for adding video content to your demos.

## Step 1: Record Your Content

Record the screen content you want to show during your demo. Common tools:

- **OBS Studio** — free, cross-platform screen recording
- **Windows Game Bar** (`Win+G`) — quick capture on Windows
- **macOS Screenshot** (`Cmd+Shift+5`) — built-in screen recording

<Aside type="tip">
Record at your screen's native resolution for the sharpest playback. Snipsy handles aspect ratio differences with letterboxing.
</Aside>

## Step 2: Import into Snipsy

<Steps>

1. Open your project and navigate to the **Videos** view
2. Click **+** to import — select your video file
3. The file is copied into your project's `videos/` directory
4. It now appears in the video library

</Steps>

## Step 3: Create a Clip

<Steps>

1. Navigate to the **Clips** view
2. Click **+** to create a new video snippet
3. Select your imported video as the source
4. Use the **clip editor** to set:
   - **Start time** — where playback begins
   - **End time** — where playback stops
   - **Preview** the trimmed range
5. Configure settings:
   - **Speed** — 2× for builds, 1× for detail work
   - **Muted** — on by default, toggle off if audio matters
   - **End behavior** — close the window or freeze on the last frame
   - **Click to play** — show the first frame and wait for a click
6. **Assign a hotkey**
7. **Save**

</Steps>

## Step 4: Fine-Tune

After creating your clip:

- **Test playback** from the clips list using the play button
- **Adjust timing** if the start/end points need tweaking
- **Try different speeds** — sometimes 1.5× feels more natural than 2×
- **Check on your target monitor** if using multi-monitor setup

## Step 5: Present

Enter demo mode and trigger your video clips with their assigned hotkeys. The video plays fullscreen, your audience sees a polished recording, and you narrate over it.

<Aside type="note">
Press **Escape** at any time during playback to dismiss the video early — useful if you need to move on faster than expected.
</Aside>

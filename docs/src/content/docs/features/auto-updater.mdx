Snipsy includes a built-in auto-updater that checks for new versions on startup.

## How It Works

1. **On launch**, Snipsy silently checks for updates in the background
2. If an update is available, a **download icon** appears in the status bar (bottom-right, next to the version number)
3. Click the icon to see **release notes** and the new version number
4. Click **Download & Install** to update — progress is shown inline
5. Once downloaded, Snipsy **relaunches** with the new version

## Update Indicator

The update indicator appears as a small download icon with a pulsing accent dot. It's intentionally subtle — it won't interrupt your workflow or your demo.

## What Gets Updated

The updater replaces the entire application binary. Your **projects and data are untouched** — they live in your project folders, not inside the app.

<Aside type="note">
Updates are signed and verified before installation. The update mechanism uses Tauri's built-in updater plugin with code signing to ensure authenticity.
</Aside>

## Disabling Updates

The updater runs automatically on startup. There's no setting to disable it, but it only checks — it never downloads or installs without your explicit click.

The Hotkey Overview is the home screen when you open a project — a visual dashboard of every snippet and its assigned hotkey.

<Screenshot src="/images/hotkey-overview.png" alt="Hotkey overview dashboard" caption="The hotkey overview showing all text and video snippets with their assignments" />

## Card Layout

Each snippet appears as a card showing:

- **Title** and description
- **Hotkey badge** (e.g., `Ctrl+Shift+1`)
- **Type indicator** — text icon or film icon
- **Status icons** — delivery mode for text, mute/speed for video

## Drag to Reorder

Cards can be **dragged and dropped** to reorder them. This is purely visual — it lets you arrange your dashboard to match the flow of your presentation. The order is saved in your browser's local storage.

## Quick Actions

- **Click a card** to jump to its edit form
- **Play button** on video cards to preview playback

## Visual Indicators

| Icon | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| ⌨️ Fast-type | Text delivered keystroke by keystroke |
| 📋 Paste | Text delivered via clipboard |
| 🔇 Muted | Video audio is suppressed |
| 🔊 Unmuted | Video audio will play |
